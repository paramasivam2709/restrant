Our Story

From a Tiny Kitchen to a City Landmark

In 2011, two friends, one tandoor and a borrowed hand-cart started selling biryani outside a metro station. Fifteen years later, STACKLY is where the city celebrates — but every dish is still cooked like it's for family.

  • 3 Locations

    Mumbai, Pune and Bengaluru — with two more opening soon.

  • 140 Team Members

    Chefs, hosts, riders and dreamers — one family.

  • 9 Culinary Awards

    Including "Best Casual Dining 2025" by City Food Guide.

15+
Years of Taste

Our Mission

To make extraordinary food accessible to everyone — served with warmth, priced with honesty, and made with ingredients we'd feed our own children.

Our Vision

To become India's most loved dining brand — one memorable meal, one happy guest and one neighbourhood at a time.

The Journey

Milestones We're Proud Of

A hand-cart, 40 portions a day, sold out by 9 pm every night. The queue became the brand's first marketing.

STACKLY opens its first dine-in space: 12 tables, one tandoor and a waiting list three weekends long.

Delivery launched. We crossed 100,000 orders within the first year — with a 4.8 average rating.

Pune and Bengaluru welcomed their first STACKLY outlets. Our chef brigade grew to 25+.

Today we serve over ten thousand regulars, cater 200+ events a year, and still taste-test every new dish as a family.

Quality Standards

How We Keep Every Plate Exceptional

Farm-to-Kitchen Daily

Produce arrives before sunrise and is prepped the same day. Nothing sits overnight.

Live-Fire Cooking

Clay tandoors and wood grills — flavour you cannot fake with shortcuts.

3-Stage Taste Test

Every new recipe is tasted by chefs, staff and guest panels before it reaches the menu.
